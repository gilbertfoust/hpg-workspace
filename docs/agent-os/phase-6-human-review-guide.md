# Phase 6 Human Review Guide

Phase 6 remains `ready_for_human_review`. The twenty-one automated and security gates confirm that the implementation behaves consistently, but they do not approve organizational policy, staffing, legal interpretation, financial judgment, or executive authority.

Eight evidence-required human reviews remain.

## 1. Administration and Executive Secretariat review

Review:

- executive brief format;
- decision packet clarity;
- meeting and approval records;
- document references;
- naming and reference conventions;
- administrative routing;
- record retention implications;
- executive intake burden; and
- whether raw department material is incorrectly presented as an executive-ready conclusion.

Confirm that Noemi provides one synthesized executive view while detailed source material remains in the responsible department workspace.

## 2. Operations and department-routing review

Review:

- thirteen module profiles;
- lead-agent assignments;
- routing-agent assignments;
- existing board bindings;
- native agent-work boards;
- assignment status lifecycle;
- priority and risk calculations;
- duplicate prevention;
- cross-department ownership; and
- assignment volume.

Confirm that the Agent OS assignment does not change the human owner or execute the source work.

## 3. Program and NGO Coordination review

Review:

- NGO Coordination ownership;
- Program and Curriculum boundaries;
- safeguarding and program-risk treatment;
- NGO portfolio context;
- reporting requirements;
- activation boundaries;
- partner visibility; and
- separation between internal Agent OS work and external NGO portal access.

Confirm that external NGO users receive no Phase 6 internal operating or executive information.

## 4. Finance and Development review

Review:

- financial-risk decision candidates;
- grant-action decision candidates;
- grant options and consequences;
- evidence-strength calculations;
- readiness and priority scores;
- budget, expense, journal, forecast, and grant data limitations;
- Finance and Development confidentiality; and
- resource-commitment language.

Confirm that Phase 6 does not post, approve, pay, transfer, submit, or commit HPG resources automatically.

## 5. General Counsel, privacy, and governance review

Review:

- confidentiality levels;
- legal terminology;
- policy and Board boundaries;
- decision evidence;
- institutional-memory use;
- source snapshots;
- personal data exposure;
- executive decision retention;
- dissent preservation;
- waiver authority; and
- source-record access areas.

Confirm that Phase 6 does not make a legal conclusion, policy determination, or Board decision.

## 6. Technology validation

Review:

- protected thirty-minute schedule;
- advisory concurrency lock;
- refresh failure recovery;
- assignment and decision idempotency;
- stable fingerprints;
- RLS;
- direct-write denial;
- public RPC security;
- external persona isolation;
- event ordering;
- event-chain hashes;
- view performance;
- backup and recovery; and
- production verification script.

## 7. Efficiency and model-risk quality assurance

Review:

- assignment relevance;
- false-positive assignments;
- department score sensitivity;
- over-alerting;
- decision-packet duplication;
- capacity-decision aggregation;
- option quality;
- recommendation bias;
- dissent visibility;
- evidence-strength calibration;
- priority ordering;
- missing-data limitations;
- workload created by the control center; and
- whether a high score could be mistaken for a human performance assessment.

Efficiency should return any score, rule, or packet that adds noise without improving executive judgment.

## 8. Chief Executive authorization

Review the complete evidence packet after the preceding reviews.

Executive authorization should consider:

- whether the command center provides useful synthesis;
- whether the queue reflects genuine CEO-level matters;
- whether department leaders can challenge assignments and recommendations;
- whether limitations are visible;
- whether dissent survives synthesis;
- whether the operating burden is proportionate;
- whether human authority is unmistakable; and
- whether the system is ready for controlled pilot use.

Authorization does not enable:

```text
external actions
autonomous decisions
source mutations
agent execution
financial execution
grant submission
legal conclusions
Board decisions
employment decisions
```

## Decision requirements

A pass or waiver requires:

- authenticated reviewer;
- written review findings;
- evidence reference;
- review timestamp;
- completion of the associated Workspace item; and
- no unresolved failed security gate.

A waiver requires super-administrator authority. Executive authorization may be recorded only by the named CEO.

A failed human gate pauses the program until the issue is corrected and reviewed again.
