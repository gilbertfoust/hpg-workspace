## Interpretation limits

The alert queue reflects the connected authoritative records and the current controlled rules. It should not be interpreted as proof that an unalerted area has no risk. Examples include:

- incomplete source data;
- unrecorded commitments;
- external deadlines not yet entered into the Workspace;
- financial accounts or forecasts not yet populated; and
- conditions outside the current thirty-one-rule library.

Human reviewers should examine both the alerts and the source-coverage view before making an operational or governance decision.
